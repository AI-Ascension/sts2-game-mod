# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respectful communication,
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks,
publication of private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify, edit, or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions
that violate this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the security contact
described in SECURITY.md. We will protect the reporter's confidentiality as far as practical.
